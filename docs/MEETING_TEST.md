# Tonight's meeting: 20–25 minute concept test

1. Give each tester the hub on a phone or laptop. Say only: “Choose a game and play.” Do not coach them through it.
2. Allocate roughly two to three minutes per prototype. Rotate the order across testers: quiz → fantasy → mystery; fantasy → mystery → quiz; mystery → quiz → fantasy.
3. Observe the first action, requests for help, completion, and whether the explanation is read. Ask: “What did you learn about a player?”
4. On each result screen, enter a nickname and rate fun, clarity and desire to replay from 1 to 5. Add one concrete improvement.
5. Open Test notebook. Export from each device and compare responses manually. Do not compare raw game scores across concepts.
6. Include at least one younger and one older tester. Record whether they needed help, could explain the reveal, and chose another round. Do not assume that age predicts hockey knowledge.
7. Ask each person to choose just one concept to develop. Resolve disagreements using observed confusion, remembered learning and voluntary replay.

Suggested selection bar for this small exploratory test: most testers finish without help, can explain one metric in their own words, and would choose another round. Ratings are directional evidence, not a statistically meaningful experiment.

## Walkthroughs for the facilitator

- **Quiz:** open Scout the players first if unfamiliar with the fictional match. Complete five choices. Check that each reveal names the leader and explains the metric. Finish, rate, replay.
- **Fantasy:** inspect scoring, choose three affordable players, remove one and replace them, lock the lineup. See per-player calculation and optimum. Finish and rate.
- **Mystery:** inspect the roster, guess a player, use another clue if needed, solve or exhaust four guesses. Check point deductions, then try a practice puzzle and rate.

## Activation concept

- Quiz: a small post-period card beside the results, then a reveal linking back to player stats.
- Fantasy: a pre-game three-player draft, with points refreshed after each period; the present demo tests the retrospective draft loop only.
- Mystery: a daily social teaser linking to a mobile web puzzle, with shareable result text after privacy and data permissions are agreed.

## New-game walkthroughs

- **Spot the Difference:** watch the animated lead-in, let it freeze, choose one of three players, watch the continuation and explanation. Try pause, replay, the position slider and a wrong answer. Reduced-motion users can inspect the frames without automatic movement. Complete three rounds and rate.
- **Chain Reaction:** tap three event cards in order, change a filled slot, check the sequence, then replay it. Make two wrong attempts to check the optional reveal. Complete three rounds and rate.
- **Chance or Luck?:** choose the better chance from two shot diagrams. Compare the out-of-100 dots with what happened this time. Complete three rounds and explain why a goal does not prove a shot was the better opportunity.

All three additions use authored synthetic scenarios. Importing the real player table affects games 1–3 only. For the age-diverse test, try no facilitator explanation beyond the on-screen instructions; gather ratings independently before discussing.
